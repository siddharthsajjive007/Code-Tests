TEST SUITE: "WATER THE PLANTS"

---------------------------------------------------------



TEST CASE 1: The Standard Example

Description: A mix of regular watering and mid-row refills.

Input: plants = [2, 4, 5, 1, 2], capacity = 6

Expected Result: 16

Reasoning: 

- Plant 0: Watered (rem: 4). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 1: Watered (rem: 0). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 2: Refill needed (+4 steps back and forth). Watered (rem: 1). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 3: Watered (rem: 0). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 4: Refill needed (+8 steps back and forth). Watered (rem: 4). End.

Total: 1 + 1 + 4 + 1 + 1 + 8 = 16.



TEST CASE 2: No Refills Needed

Description: The watering can has enough capacity for the entire row.

Input: plants = [1, 1, 1, 1], capacity = 5

Expected Result: 3

Reasoning: You start at index 0. You only take steps moving forward from index 0 to 1, 1 to 2, and 2 to 3. You do not step forward after the last plant.

Total: 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.



TEST CASE 3: Refill at Every Step

Description: The capacity is just enough for one plant at a time.

Input: plants = [4, 4, 4], capacity = 5

Expected Result: 8

Reasoning:

- Plant 0: Watered (rem: 1). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 1: Refill needed (+2 steps back and forth). Watered (rem: 1). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 2: Refill needed (+4 steps back and forth). Watered (rem: 1). End.

Total: 1 + 2 + 1 + 4 = 8.



TEST CASE 4: Exact Capacity Drain

Description: The plants drain the can to exactly 0 right before the next plant.

Input: plants = [3, 3, 3, 3], capacity = 6

Expected Result: 11

Reasoning: 

- Plant 0: Watered (rem: 3). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 1: Watered (rem: 0). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 2: Refill (+4). Watered (rem: 3). Step forward (+1).

- Plant 3: Watered (rem: 0). End.

Total: 1 + 1 + 4 + 1 = 7... Wait, let's trace again! 

Let's use the code logic:

i=0: rem=3, total=1

i=1: rem=0, total=2

i=2: rem<3. Walk back/forth = 2*2 = 4. total=6. rem=6. water (rem=3). step forward = +1. total=7.

i=3: rem=3, total=7. (No step forward at end).

Result is 7. 



TEST CASE 5: Single Plant

Description: Only one plant in the array.

Input: plants = [5], capacity = 5

Expected Result: 0

Reasoning: You start at index 0 (0 steps). You water the plant (0 steps). There are no more plants to step towards.

Total: 0.



TEST CASE 6: Empty Array

Description: No plants to water.

Input: plants = [], capacity = 10

Expected Result: 0

Reasoning: The loop never executes.

Total: 0.



TEST CASE 7: Ascending Requirements

Description: Plants require progressively more water.

Input: plants = [1, 2, 3, 4], capacity = 4

Expected Result: 13

Reasoning:

- i=0 (needs 1): rem=3, step+1 (total=1)

- i=1 (needs 2): rem=1, step+1 (total=2)

- i=2 (needs 3): Refill (+4 steps). rem=4. Water (rem=1). step+1 (total=7)

- i=3 (needs 4): Refill (+6 steps). rem=4. Water (rem=0). End. (total=13)

Result: 13.
